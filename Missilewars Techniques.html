Missilewars Mechanics and Tricks

Hi, I’m Hannah_void!
This is a collection of mechanics and tricks for Missilewars. If you are an experienced player reading this, you will probably be aware of most of the things I will write about, but there might be a few things you didn’t know amongst them.

This should mainly help new players get familiar with the game. I will also write a similar document about strategies soon.

1. Using the bow in melee

A common mistake I see new players do is punching enemies that they want to kill with their fists. You spawn in with a bow called “Gunblade”. The bow has the sharpness enchant and will as such do more damage than your fist if you punch people with it.

2. Shieldtowers

When riding your missile across you almost always want as much altitude as possible.
To do that, place a shield in your base and mine some of its block in such a ways (e.g.: staircase) that you can get up.

Optimal shield placement:
You can get 5 blocks of height per shield.
So two shields get you 10.
Most people only get 4 per shield.

To place the shield, look straight down, jump, and throw the shield at the highest point during your jump. The snowball that spawns a shield will now collide with your player hitbox instead of the ground, therefore spawning higher.

3. Cannons

Another way to increase your altitude, is by throwing a cannon upwards (throw while jumping so the projectile flies further) and then breaking some of the blocks so the water flows down. You can swim up the water stream.

4. Item timer

Knowing when you get the next item can help.

Players get an item every 10 seconds, the first item is given 3 seconds after game start. So whenever the in-game timer ends with a seven, you get an item.

5. Igniting a stuck missile

When you successfully get to the enemy base and want to destroy the portal, you might find that the missile you placed is close enough to the portal to destroy it, if it were to explode.
However a single missile lodged into the glass rarely ignites on its own.

In order to ignite it, you could place another missile in the same spot. Since missiles contain redstone blocks, you will be able to ignite the tnt of the first missile in most cases.

In order to get it to explode faster, throw a grenade or fireball at it. The time difference from this is often critical in a 1v1.

6. Using a shield to gain horizontal distance

Sometimes you don’t want altitude to get on top of the enemy base, but rather want to do a side attack in which case you need to get far enough to the side that you can fly a missile past the enemy base. Placing a shield on the side of your base helps with that.

7. Blocking with a shield

A shield can be used to stop an enemy missile. The usual approach to throw a shield in front of a missile works, but only sometimes. Some missiles can go through shields and wont be stopped. Even if a missile gets stopped, a player riding it can climb over the shield and place a new missile or even use it as the base for a shield tower.

A better way is to make use of the shield replacing blocks as it spawns. By throwing the shield on the pistons of the missile, the pistons will get replaced by glass from the shield and wont fly anymore.

8. Shooting missiles with a bow

When trying to make an enemy missile explode, don’t just shoot any tnt. The tnt that you aim for should not be able to fall down once ignited and also not come in contact with a moving slime block or it will be catapulted away.

The later can be useful though, if an opponent jumped from their original missile onto a new one, you can make the old missile launch a tnt on the new one.

9. Shooting players

You need only 3 hits to kill a player reliably with a bow. Make sure at least two of your shots are at full strength and wait 2 seconds before shots to maximize fire damage. This leaves you with 2 arrows that you can use elsewhere.

10. Deflecting fireballs

Fireballs can be reflected. Spam clicking gives you a solid chance at hitting the fireball in time, but you have a couple ticks in between punches where the game won’t register if you hit the fireball. Therefore timing the punch is the most consistent way to deflect a fireball.

Sadly ping matters a lot here, so to maximise your chance at deflecting the fireball, try to hit it with a projectile if possible. Arrows, other fireballs, grenades, and shields all work.

Fireballs are affected by explosions and can be knocked off-course if something explodes nearby.

Fireballs can also be pushed away by moving slime blocks, but only if the slime moves at the right moment into the fireball. As such, this is unreliable, but still a reason why you should not remove the slimeblocks on a “shielder” missile.

11. How to throw a grenade far

Most people seem to underestimate how far you can throw a grenade. The distance you can throw it is almost the entire map. To throw a grenade properly, sprint jump and throw the grenade while ascending.
(You can walk backwards immediately after throwing it, to reduce the distance you as a player travel forwards.)

12. Using missiles to gain horizontal distance

If you want to get some horizontal distance, e.g. for a side-rush, you don’t necessarily need a shield, just a few missiles, preferably 3 wide.

Most missiles are 3 blocks wide. This gives them a center, a right side and a left side. By placing a new missile while looking at one of the side blocks, you spawn a missile a bit more to the side than the previous missile. If you repeat this, you can get a lot of horizontal distance.

When doing this with 2 wide missiles, you need to be carefull. For red, this technique only works if you want to gain distance to the left. For blue its to the right.

13. Missile parkour

Riding a missile is fun!

What is not fun, is seeing that in the lane that you are flying in is some sort of obstruction that will stop you or more likely make your missile explode.

To get around this, just place a new missiles while riding the first and jump onto the new missile. Beware of honey blocks or slime blocks messing with your jump.

Repeatedly jumping on a missile you placed and placing a new one to jump to gets you across faster then just riding a single missile. However you loose altitude quickly as every missile spawns a bit lower than the last, so don’t do this if you want to be able to get on top of the enemy base.

14. Missile bridge

Sometimes missiles with low amounts of tnt leave behind a few blocks after they exploded. These blocks can sometimes be usefull. You can also place a missile and then destroy some slime so it stops midair. With this it is possible to make bridges. Since sprint jumping is faster than any missile flies, you can sometimes save a couple of seconds when trying to get across to the enemy base.

15. Disarming your own missiles

If the missile you are riding is hit by a grenade or fireball, it will most likely explode and kill you. If you break some of the tnt blocks on it, you increase the chance of your missile taking a hit unscathed.

Most missiles will explode upon hitting an obstacle. By removing specific tnt, you can make it so your missile will safely stop upon hitting an unmovable object. This is great when you want to arrive at the enemy base and then mine in. After all, if the missile you used to get there were to explode behind you it could mess you up.

16. Missilestacking

If you are close to the enemy portal, maybe even on top of there base or similar, you want to either create as big of an explosion as you can, or have the furthest tnt spread (to hit multiple portals at once). Both can be done by placing multiple missiles in almost the same spot.

17. Controlling explosion spread

If there is a big explosion (2 heavy missiles collide, etc.) there will often be tnt flying in all directions, often hitting missiles that were flying relatively far away.

The direction in which tnt flies in such a circumstance is dependent on where the first explosion in the chain reaction happened. If you are worried about a big missile exploding near you, make it explode yourself! Shoot the tnt closest to you and watch as the missile explodes and tnt flies in the opposite direction to you. (Hopefully.)

18. Build height

At some altitude, shields, platforms and cannons won’t spawn anymore, but you can still place missiles or blocks. If you use blocks to get even higher, you will enter a zone where all blocks are invincible. You won’t be able to break blocks you placed there. They also become resistant to explosions. The same applies to missiles in that area.

This makes it possible to soft-lock a player, if you manage to encase them at such a height. They wont be able to break out no matter what. Their only option is to leave the game or watch from their prison as the game continues without them.

19. Using platforms to stun enemies

Platforms are very versatile. If you throw a platform at an enemies head, they will be forced in crawl-mode. Crawling is slow, meaning they will be stuck under the platform for a while.

You can also use platforms to get yourself in crawl-mode, which is useful for tunneling inside the enemy base.

20. Fireballs and grenades as a staircase

If you want to get on top of the enemy base, but will arrive a bit too low, you can throw a grenade or fireball at the edge (preferably corner) of the base, destroying a few block and making it easier to get up.

22. Map specific knowledge

On thick, siderushs are the easiest, due to the portal having almost the same size as the base is wide.

On wall, you get an easy start to a siderush since the wall in the middle is wider than the bases.

On bastion, you can mine a stoneblock by hand of one of the crennelations to get a two block height advantage.

On bastion, If you want to mine through, go for the lower windows. Its easier to block defenders.

On bastion, if you mined all the way through a window, you can get both portals at once by placing a few missiles on the backside so they are lodged into another and wont fly away.

On Highrise, you can use the launchpads to get on the rooftops and ride a missile from there for increased elevation. This is enough to make in on top of the enemy base.

On Highrise, portals are more easily destroyed from the backside, because portals are not centered inside the buildings.
